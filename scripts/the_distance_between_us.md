# THE DISTANCE BETWEEN US

## BaseBoard Demo Script

### Genre
Contemporary Romance • Drama

### Theme
Love is not diminished by distance — it is tested, and proven.

---

## World Notes

Present-day Toronto and Tokyo. Two cities separated by 13 time zones.

**Toronto, Canada:** Emma's world. Exposed-brick art studios, late-night diners,
university library stacks. A city of grey skies and warm interiors. She photographs
everything and draws everything she cannot photograph.

**Tokyo, Japan:** Kai's world. Robotics lab corridors, cherry blossom parks, small
shrine walkways, the hum of a city that never fully sleeps. He works with his hands
and thinks with precision, but keeps a poet's notebook on his desk.

The long-distance relationship between Emma and Kai spans one year. Video calls,
time-zone math, care packages, and the slow accumulation of small rituals that
become the architecture of a relationship.

Lucas is Emma's oldest friend — her flatmate, confidant, and the person who sees
what she refuses to admit. He carries his vintage camera everywhere and documents
the city's forgotten corners.

---

## Factions / Social Groups

### The Studio Circle
Emma's art-school friends. They gather at the studio on Friday nights. They like
Kai but worry that Emma is putting her life on pause.

### The Robotics Lab
Kai's team at the university. Smart, focused, deadline-driven. They know Kai is
distracted. They do not ask why.

## Artifacts

### 1. Shared Sketchbook (Primary Artifact)
**Owner:** Emma & Kai
Emma starts the sketchbook in Toronto — charcoal portraits, loose street scenes,
margin poems. Kai receives it by post and adds robotics sketches, wiring diagrams
with handwritten captions, love notes pressed between pages.
By the final scene it is a full object: part diary, part blueprint, part love letter.
**Appears in:** Images 1, 2, 9, 11, 15

### 2. Sunrise Polaroid
**Owner:** Kai
Emma photographs a Tokyo sunrise from her hotel window during an early visit and
sends the print to Kai. He tapes it above his monitor. Later it travels inside his
passport holder.
**Appears in:** Images 2, 6, 11

### 3. Vintage Camera
**Owner:** Lucas
Lucas's 35mm film camera — battered, reliable, always loaded. It captures the
decisive moments: Emma leaving for the airport, Kai's arrival at Pearson
International, the reunion embrace. At the end Lucas photographs Emma and Kai
together, lowering the camera and smiling.
**Appears in:** Images 3, 5, 10, 15

### 4. Cherry Blossom Wish Ribbon
**Owner:** Kai
Tied at a Tokyo shrine. The handwritten wish: *"Bring us together."*
Kai unties it before boarding his flight to Canada and folds it inside the
shared sketchbook.
**Appears in:** Images 8, 11, 15

### 5. Boarding Pass
**Owner:** Kai
Several tickets accumulate — cancelled, rebooked, postponed. The final boarding
pass is used. It represents the decision to stop waiting and choose.
**Appears in:** Images 6, 11, 13

### 6. Compass Pendant
**Owner:** Emma
A small silver compass on a delicate chain — a first-anniversary gift from Kai.
Whenever Emma doubts whether the distance is worth it, she reaches for it.
At the reunion Kai notices immediately. She never took it off.
**Appears in:** Images 1, 4, 9, 13, 15

## Characters

### Emma Clarke
Age: 26
Origin: Toronto, Canada
Profession: Graphic Design student / part-time studio assistant
Personality: Creative, observant, quietly stubborn, fiercely loyal.
Appearance: Warm medium-brown skin, natural hair usually pinned up with a pencil
or charcoal stick, paint-stained fingers, wire-frame glasses. Wears layered knits
and vintage denim. Compass pendant always visible at the throat.
Arc: Learns to trust the future she has built rather than the certainty she craves.

### Kai Tanaka
Age: 27
Origin: Tokyo, Japan
Profession: Robotics engineer, university research lab
Personality: Methodical, tender, quietly funny. Thinks in systems but feels deeply.
Appearance: Japanese, lean build, dark hair grown slightly too long, often has
grease or solder marks on his hands. Wears plain technical tees under open flannels.
Carries the sunrise polaroid in his passport holder.
Arc: Learns that commitment is not a sacrifice — it is a choice made freely.

### Lucas Ferreira
Age: 26
Origin: Toronto, Canada (Brazilian heritage)
Profession: Documentary photography student
Personality: Perceptive, warm, self-deprecating. Fiercely protective of Emma
while also honest enough to challenge her.
Appearance: Brazilian-Canadian, curly dark hair, always a camera strap over one
shoulder, well-worn canvas jacket, laugh lines already forming.
Arc: Learns to let Emma go and trust that she has chosen well.

## Screenplay

---

### SCENE 1 — A WORLD IN DRAWINGS

INT. EMMA'S STUDIO APARTMENT — TORONTO — EVENING — IMAGE 1

The apartment is small and full of evidence of a life in progress: half-finished
canvases, string lights, library books stacked by the radiator.

Emma sits cross-legged on the studio floor, the SHARED SKETCHBOOK open on her lap.
She is drawing from memory — Kai's hands, the shape of his wrist, the way he holds
a circuit board.

The COMPASS PENDANT catches the light.

EMMA (V.O.)
The distance is a fact. Thirteen time zones. Eight thousand kilometres.
One sketchbook that goes back and forth in the mail like a slow conversation.

She finishes the drawing, holds the sketchbook up to the window, then smiles.

---

### SCENE 2 — PARALLEL DESKS

INT. KAI'S LAB — TOKYO — SAME NIGHT (TOKYO MORNING) — IMAGE 2

Kai is at his workbench, robotics components spread before him. The SUNRISE POLAROID
is taped to the monitor. The SHARED SKETCHBOOK sits open beside his keyboard —
Emma's charcoal drawings visible on the left page, Kai's own neat pen annotations
filling the right.

KAI
(reading aloud from the sketchbook)
"You left your coffee mug in my studio. I use it every morning. Don't ask me to
return it."

He laughs quietly. He writes a reply in the margin.

---

### SCENE 3 — THE KEEPER OF EVIDENCE

EXT. TORONTO STREETS — AFTERNOON — IMAGE 3

Lucas walks beside Emma through the university district, VINTAGE CAMERA swinging
from his neck.

LUCAS
You're using the present tense less.

EMMA
What?

LUCAS
When you talk about him. "Kai is." You used to say "Kai is." Now you say
"Kai was" or "Kai would."

Emma stops walking.

EMMA
That is not true.

LUCAS
(gently)
I have a photograph of your face when you say his name. I have seventeen of them.
You want to see the difference between January and now?

Emma does not answer. Lucas lifts the camera and photographs her anyway.

---

### SCENE 4 — THE PENDANT

INT. EMMA'S STUDIO — NIGHT — IMAGE 4

Emma is on a video call with Kai. The connection keeps buffering. They have already
tried twice. She is not angry — she is tired in a way she cannot name.

Her hand moves to the COMPASS PENDANT.

EMMA
I don't think distance gets easier. I think you just get better at carrying it.

KAI
(from the screen)
Is that a good thing?

EMMA
I don't know yet.

They sit in silence for a moment. The kind of silence that is its own form of
closeness.

KAI
I'm working on something. Give me until the end of the month.

Emma nods. She does not ask what.

---

### SCENE 5 — WHAT LUCAS SEES

INT. PHOTO LAB — TORONTO — AFTERNOON — IMAGE 5

Lucas develops his film prints in the university darkroom. The VINTAGE CAMERA sits
on the counter.

As images emerge in the developing tray, we see: Emma at the window. Emma holding
the pendant. Emma laughing. Emma staring at her phone.

Lucas pins the prints to the wire. He steps back and looks at all of them together.

He picks up the phone. He calls Kai.

LUCAS
She's fine. She's not fine. You should know both things.

---

### SCENE 6 — THE TICKET

INT. KAI'S APARTMENT — TOKYO — NIGHT — IMAGE 6

Kai is at his kitchen table. His laptop shows a flight booking confirmation.
The BOARDING PASS is printing. The SUNRISE POLAROID is propped against the
fruit bowl, watching.

He texts Emma:
> *"I have something to tell you. Not on a call. I'll write it in the book."*

He opens the SHARED SKETCHBOOK to a fresh page and begins.

---

### SCENE 7 — THE LETTER IN THE BOOK

INT. EMMA'S STUDIO — TORONTO — DAYS LATER — IMAGE 7

Emma receives the sketchbook in the post. She sits on the floor, back against the
radiator, and reads.

Kai's entry fills three pages: the robotics diagrams give way to words. He writes
about the polaroid on his desk. He writes about the compass pendant he chose for
her and what it was supposed to mean. He writes about what he wants next.

The last line reads:
> *"I am not asking you to wait. I am telling you I am coming."*

Emma reads it twice. Then she closes the book and holds it against her chest.

---

### SCENE 8 — THE SHRINE

EXT. TOKYO SHRINE — EARLY MORNING — IMAGE 8

Kai stands alone at a small neighbourhood shrine in the pre-dawn quiet.

He ties a WISH RIBBON to the wooden frame. In careful handwriting:
> *"Bring us together."*

He photographs it on his phone. Then he stands for a long time, hands in his
pockets, watching the ribbon move in the breeze.

---

### SCENE 9 — EMMA ANSWERS

INT. EMMA'S STUDIO — TORONTO — EVENING — IMAGE 9

Emma sits at her desk with the SHARED SKETCHBOOK open to a new page. She is drawing
Kai from the most recent photo she has of him — working in the lab, not posing,
not looking at the camera.

The COMPASS PENDANT rests on the desk beside her sketchbook. She picks it up,
considers it, then puts it back on.

She writes beneath the drawing:
> *"Then come."*

---

### SCENE 10 — LUCAS AT THE AIRPORT

EXT. PEARSON INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT — TORONTO — MORNING — IMAGE 10

Emma and Lucas stand at the arrivals barrier. Lucas has the VINTAGE CAMERA out but
has not raised it.

EMMA
Put the camera away.

LUCAS
I'm documenting history.

EMMA
Lucas.

He lowers it. But keeps it ready.

EMMA
Thank you. For the phone call.

LUCAS
(shrugs)
He needed to know. You needed him to know that I knew.

The arrivals doors open.

---

### SCENE 11 — ARRIVAL

INT. PEARSON INTERNATIONAL — ARRIVALS HALL — IMAGE 11

Kai comes through the doors. He is carrying a shoulder bag and looks slightly
rumpled from the fourteen-hour flight.

He has the SHARED SKETCHBOOK under one arm. The BOARDING PASS is folded inside it.
The WISH RIBBON, now untied from the shrine, is folded in the front pocket.

The SUNRISE POLAROID is in his shirt pocket. He does not know Emma will ask about
it and he will produce it immediately, slightly embarrassed, and she will not be
embarrassed at all.

They embrace. Neither says anything for a long moment.

Behind them, Lucas raises the VINTAGE CAMERA and takes the photograph.

---

### SCENE 12 — THE SKETCHBOOK, COMPLETE

INT. EMMA'S STUDIO — LATER THAT EVENING — IMAGE 12

They sit together on the studio floor with the SHARED SKETCHBOOK open between them.
Kai's pages and Emma's pages — layouts and charcoal, circuits and portraits.

KAI
(pointing to his robotics diagram)
This is the first thing I drew after I received it. I didn't know what to add.
So I explained what I was building.

EMMA
(pointing to her drawing of his hands)
This is the first thing I drew after I sent it. I was already missing you.

They turn pages together slowly.

---

### SCENE 13 — THE DECISION

INT. EMMA'S KITCHEN — MORNING — IMAGE 13

Kai makes coffee. Emma sits on the counter watching him.

The COMPASS PENDANT is visible at her throat.

EMMA
You can't stay in my studio forever.

KAI
I know.

EMMA
So what happens next?

Kai sets down the coffee. He reaches into his bag and produces the final BOARDING
PASS — not the arrival ticket. A different one. A one-way ticket.

KAI
There's a research position. I applied three months ago. I found out last week.
I wanted to tell you in person.

Emma looks at the ticket for a long time.

EMMA
You were going to come regardless.

KAI
Yes.

EMMA
(quietly)
Okay then.

---

### SCENE 14 — LUCAS DEVELOPS THE LAST ROLL

INT. PHOTO LAB — TORONTO — IMAGE 14

Lucas develops the airport roll. The image emerges in the tray: Emma and Kai
in the arrivals hall, mid-embrace, blurred at the edges from motion.

Lucas pins it to the wire. He steps back.

He looks at it for a while.

Then he picks up the VINTAGE CAMERA and reloads it with fresh film.

---

### SCENE 15 — EVERYTHING THAT REMAINS

EXT. TORONTO WATERFRONT — GOLDEN HOUR — IMAGE 15

Emma, Kai, and Lucas walk along the waterfront. The city skyline reflects in the
lake. The light is the kind that makes everything look like a memory while it is
still happening.

Lucas stops, raises the VINTAGE CAMERA, and photographs them together.

Emma is holding Kai's hand. The COMPASS PENDANT catches the late sun.

The SHARED SKETCHBOOK is in Kai's bag. The WISH RIBBON is tucked inside it.

Lucas lowers the camera and keeps walking.

LUCAS (V.O.)
I used to think the distance was the story. All those kilometres, all those
time zones. But the distance was never the story. It was just the weather.

The three of them walk into the golden light.

FADE OUT.
