# THE LOST COMPASS

## BaseBoard Demo Script

### Genre
Sci-Fi Adventure • Mystery

### Theme
Knowledge is humanity's greatest treasure.

---

## World Lore

In the year **2248**, Earth has become a network of futuristic megacities connected by orbital transit gates.

Thousands of years ago, an ancient civilization called **The Cartographers** hid humanity's greatest knowledge inside the legendary **Vault of Humanity**.

The vault can only be opened when four worthy guardians unite the four Compass Fragments.

Meanwhile, a dangerous faction known as **The Obsidian Circle** seeks the vault to rewrite history and control the future.

## Screenplay

---

### Scene 1 — The Calling

LOCATIONS: Neo Accra (Africa) / Neo Nairobi (Africa) / Neo Mexico City (North America) / Neo New York (North America)

Across four continents, four strangers simultaneously discover mysterious glowing Compass Fragments hidden within family heirlooms, ancient ruins, forgotten temples, and hover-racing trophies.

Each fragment projects the same holographic message:

FRAGMENT (V.O.)
"The Vault awakens. Seek Cairo."

---

### Scene 2 — Strangers Become Rivals

LOCATION: Neo Cairo, Egypt — beneath the Great Pyramid

The four strangers meet for the first time. No one trusts anyone.

HARRISON
(mocking)
You're going to slow us all down, tortoise.

THEO
(quietly)
Speed isn't always the answer.

AMINA
I'm not working with someone whose only skill is running away from problems.

BELLA
(stepping between them)
We were all called here for a reason. Let's at least find out why.

Amina cross-references the Compass Fragments against the Atlas Codex on a holographic interface.

AMINA
The fragments are linked. Four unique resonance signatures — one per person in this room. We descend from Cartographer guardians.

They discover the Atlas Codex beneath the pyramid floor, confirming that each of them was chosen.

Suddenly, drones from the Obsidian Circle attack. The group barely escapes through a hidden tunnel.

---

### Scene 3 — The Journey

LOCATIONS: Neo Rome, Italy / Swiss Alps

Using orbital transit gates, the team travels from Neo Cairo through Neo Rome and into the Swiss Alps.

Inside a hidden Cartographer archive embedded in an Alpine cliff face, they recover the Chrono Hourglass. It activates, projecting visions of the first guardians and the true purpose of the Vault: to preserve humanity's collective knowledge — not riches.

That evening, around a wooden table in a mountain village, tensions soften over a shared meal.

BELLA
(quietly, to Theo)
You don't say much.

THEO
I've learned that listening matters more.

Bella smiles. Across the table, Harrison still believes speed alone will win.

HARRISON
We've wasted two days talking. We should already be at the next fragment.

AMINA
(dryly)
You've never actually worked with a team before, have you?

---

### Scene 4 — The Trial

LOCATION: Neo Kyoto, Japan — ancient bamboo sanctuary

Inside a sacred Cartographer sanctuary preserved under a climate dome, the group faces mechanical traps and impossible puzzles left by the first guardians.

The only way forward requires all four Compass Fragments to activate simultaneously — every person must commit at exactly the same moment.

AMINA
The synchronisation requires complete trust. One hesitation and the mechanism resets.

HARRISON
(frustrated)
This is insane. We barely know each other.

THEO
(firm, calm)
We don't need to know each other. We need to trust each other. Just this once.

Another argument erupts. Theo steps forward, speaks quietly but with complete conviction — and one by one, the others fall silent and listen.

The four fragments glow. The vault door opens.

Seconds later, a bridge section collapses beneath Bella's feet. Without hesitation, Theo throws himself forward and catches her.

For the first time, the group is truly a team.

---

### Scene 5 — The Betrayal

LOCATION: Orbital Transit Hub, above the Atlantic

The Obsidian Circle, led by Seraphis, ambushes the group mid-transit.

Seraphis steps forward, hood fanned, obsidian armor catching the light.

SERAPHIS
Harrison Brooks. You've spent your whole life chasing recognition. I can give you fame, power, a place in history. Hand me the Compass.

Harrison looks at Seraphis. Then at his team.

HARRISON
(beat)
Not a chance.

The team fights off the Obsidian Circle together, protecting both the artifacts and one another. Seraphis retreats.

---

### Scene 6 — The Final Vault

LOCATION: Neo New York — Observatory at the tip of the Manhattan Skyspire

The four artifacts are brought together:
- Compass of Unity
- Atlas Codex
- Chrono Hourglass
- Four Compass Fragments

The Vault of Humanity opens.

There is no gold. No weapons. No power source.

Instead: humanity's greatest achievements — scientific discoveries, medical breakthroughs, ancient maps, languages, art, music, engineering, history — everything the species has ever learned, preserved intact.

AMINA
(breathless)
This was never treasure. It was a library.

THEO
It belongs to everyone.

The group chooses to broadcast it freely to the world.

---

### Scene 7 — A New Beginning

LOCATION: Neo New York — rooftop restaurant, six months later

The friends reunite for dinner overlooking the futuristic skyline. Holographic displays across the city show the Vault's knowledge being freely shared — schools rebuilt, diseases cured, languages revived.

The Obsidian Circle has collapsed. Knowledge cannot be hidden when the whole world has it.

Harrison raises a glass.

HARRISON
I spent my whole life trying to be the fastest.

THEO
(smiling)
The journey mattered more than the finish.

Everyone laughs.

Beneath the table, Bella quietly reaches for Theo's hand.

The Compass rests at the centre of the table. Its needle glows once — and points toward the stars.

FADE TO BLACK.
